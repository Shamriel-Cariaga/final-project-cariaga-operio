# BookVerse
<!-- About your app: what does your app do? Example: "Today's carinderia menu and orders, without paper." -->

BookVerse is a simple and user-friendly website where readers can find and explore different books, browse by category, view book details, and save books by their interest.

## Problem
<!-- 1–2 sentences in bulleted form. Keep it direct. What everyday problem does your app solve? Who has this problem, and where?
Example: Carinderia owners in Manolo Fortich write orders on paper and lose track during lunch rush. -->

Readers have a hard time finding books that match their interest beacause books are available on many different websites.

Readers may also takes time to search for books and forget of the books they want to read, what they currently reading and what they have finished.

## Target Users
<!-- List 1–2 types of people who will use your app. Be specific.
Example: Carinderia owners; customers ordering for pickup -->

Book Lovers or Enthusiast - people who enjoy exploring different stories or genres.
Learners and Casual Readers - people who read books to gain kanowledge and to learn new things.

## Solution
<!-- Add your solution -->
BookVerse makes easier to find book in one website. Users can search books, browse different categories, read books details and save books to bookshelf. They can also organize their books based on reading progress.

## Main Feature
<!-- The ONE thing your app must do. If you removed everything else, what's left?
Example: Customers view today's menu; the owner adds and tracks orders. -->
Book discovery and Bookshelf - Users can browse books by category, view book details and save them to My Bookshelf to keep track their reading progress.

## Pages
<!-- List 3–5 pages and one line on what each page shows. Homepage and App page are required. -->
- Homepage: Displays the website introduction and featured content.
- App page: Shows the Book Library where users can search, browse books by category and view book details.
- My Bookshelf: Shows saved books into different status to All, Continue Reading, Want to Read, and Finished.
- Explore Books: Shows explore featured books on the website and discover books recommended by popularity.
